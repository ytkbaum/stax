# stax

> **Browser-based communication hub and organizational workspace** — consolidate every channel, organize everything into stax, surface what needs doing.
>
> ---
>
> ## Overview
>
> **stax** is a browser-based productivity platform that pulls your scattered communications — WhatsApp, SMS, email, and more — into a single unified workspace. Instead of jumping between apps, everything lives in one place where you can read, organize, act, and archive without ever leaving your browser.
>
> The core organizing metaphor is the **stack**: a visual pile of related items you can sort, reorder, and subdivide any which way. Stax works equally well as a personal inbox, a client correspondence tracker, a team task board, or a cloud library for anything you want to keep.
>
> ---
>
> ## Core Features
>
> ### Unified Communication Inbox
>
> stax connects to all your messaging channels through browser-based access — no native app installs required:
>
> - **WhatsApp** — read and manage messages in-app
> - - **SMS** — surfaced alongside other channels
>   - - **Email** — all accounts consolidated in one view
>     - - Additional channels pluggable as integrations grow
>      
>       - All incoming threads are normalized into a single feed, so you can triage across channels without context-switching.
>      
>       - ---
>
> ### Stax — Organize Into Piles
>
> Everything in stax can be organized into **stax** (stacks): flexible, visual containers you can shape to match how you think.
>
> - **Sort any which way** — by date, contact, topic, urgency, channel, or custom order
> - - **Subdivide freely** — split a stack into sub-stacks as granular as you need
>   - - **Drag-to-reorder** — rearrange items within and across stax
>     - - **Mix content types** — a single stack can hold emails, texts, notes, and files together
>      
>       - Stax are the backbone: every other feature (correspondence, to-do, library) is a specialized view built on top of them.
>      
>       - ---
>
> ### Cloud Library Piles
>
> Beyond active communications, stax doubles as a **cloud library** for reference material, saved content, and archival records.
>
> - Store anything — documents, links, notes, media, clipped content
> - - Organize into named piles, mirroring the stax mental model
>   - - Sort piles by any dimension: recency, topic, contact, project, tag
>     - - Access from any browser, anywhere — no local dependency
>      
>       - Library piles sit alongside active stax so reference material and live conversations share the same organizational vocabulary.
>      
>       - ---
>
> ### Correspondence Stacks
>
> For each contact, stax automatically aggregates **all communication history** across every channel into a single correspondence stack:
>
> - One view per person: all their emails, texts, and WhatsApp messages together, in chronological order
> - - **Subdivide further** — split a contact's stack by project, topic, date range, or thread
>   - - Full history is always at hand without hunting across separate apps
>    
>     - This makes stax a lightweight CRM for anyone who manages a meaningful volume of correspondence.
>    
>     - ---
>
> ### To-Do Stack
>
> stax surfaces a dedicated **To-Do stack** that gathers action items from across all your communications:
>
> - Anything flagged or identified as requiring follow-up lands here automatically
> - - Organized separately from archival correspondence so active obligations stay visible
>   - - Works as a single, unified task list drawn from every channel
>    
>     - ---
>
> ### Top View — Action Point Aggregator
>
> The **Top View** is a cross-cutting summary layer that gathers all action points emerging from consolidated correspondence:
>
> - Scans across all stax and channels for outstanding tasks, replies needed, and commitments made
> - - Presents a single, prioritized surface of what requires attention right now
>   - - Collapses the gap between "reading messages" and "knowing what to do next"
>    
>     - Top View is designed to be the first thing you open — a live dashboard of your actual obligations.

> > ## Design Principles
> >
> > **Browser-first** — no installs, no sync daemons, no platform lock-in. If you have a browser, you have stax.
> >
> > **Channel-agnostic** — the organizational layer is independent of the communication channel. WhatsApp messages and emails are treated identically once they're in a stack.
> >
> > **Flexible structure** — stax imposes no fixed taxonomy. You create the piles, pick the sort order, and decide what goes together.
> >
> > **Action-oriented** — the system is designed to move you from reading to doing, with Top View and Clips collapsing the gap between information and action.
